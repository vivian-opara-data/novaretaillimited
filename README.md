# novaretaillimited
A business operations intelligence project on a fictional retail company called Nova Retail Limited. This project showcases my skills on Microsoft Excel, SQL and Power BI.
NOVA RETAIL LIMITED
Business Operations Intelligence & Performance Analysis
Tools: Microsoft Excel, SQL Server, Power BI
Period analysed:  2025 ( January – December)
Project type: End-to-end business intelligence project
Dataset: Synthetic dataset
Project Overview
This is a project on a fictional Nigerian company that deals with retailing a number of products in different categories. The company is Nova Retail Limited and it operates across several branches (in six different states) and sales channels (in-store, market and online). The dataset contained all necessary information that could possibly be used for the analysis and to answer/solve business performance related questions/problems.
The aim of this project is to highlight my top-notch analysis skills on different platform and using data languages. For this project, I used just Microsoft Excel, SQL and Power BI as there was no need for SPSS, python, R or any other platform or programming language. I have offered meaningful business insights from this project from raw operational data which could help the company’s management make better informed decisions for the progress of the company.
Some Business-Related Questions
-How much revenue and gross profit did the business generate?
-Which products contributed the most to the revenue and gross profit?
-Which branches performed best financially?
-Which sales channel experienced the most cancellations, returns and complaints?
-How efficiently were customers’ orders being fulfilled?
-What areas required the management’s attention?
These questions and a lot more were made easy to be extensively answered in the analysis report and visualizations using extra worksheets, pivot tables, summaries, metrics and KPIs and an initial guiding worksheet in Microsoft Excel, data manipulations, aggregations and calculations using SQL query files and proper in-depth visualizations and extensive summaries in Power BI. The initial tables were cleaned, scanned through for duplicates, missing values, wrong data types and every other thing and still left as separate tables in separate worksheets in Microsoft Excel.

Key Business Insights
The analysis produced several findings:
1. Ibadan Central was the strongest financial performer across the six branches.
2. Abuja Central was the second-largest contributor to gross profit.
3. Port Harcourt maintained a relatively strong profit margin despite recording the lowest net revenue.
4. Enugu's comparatively low gross profit margin warrants further investigation.
5. Product performance varied: the highest-revenue product was not necessarily among the highest-margin products.
6. Operational performance differed across sales channels, indicating areas where order fulfilment and customer experience could be improved.
Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following actions are recommended:

- Investigate the operational practices contributing to Ibadan Central's strong performance.
- Review Enugu's product mix, pricing and discounting practices.
- Examine Port Harcourt's opportunities for revenue growth without sacrificing its margin.
- Investigate the causes of online cancellations and complaints.
- Review marketplace return patterns and identify preventable causes.
- Evaluate high-margin products with lower revenue for possible growth opportunities.

These recommendations require further investigation by the management before implementation.

Project Limitations

- The dataset is synthetic and does not represent an actual company's financial records.
- Gross profit excludes operating expenses and other costs.
- Financial analysis was based on delivered orders; returned orders were examined separately in the operational analysis.
- The findings describe the generated dataset and should not be treated as evidence of actual Nigerian retail market behaviour.
